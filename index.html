Your Money Stays Yours

  • It Can't Move Money The bot has no way to transfer, withdraw or deposit. It only places stock buys and sells.
  • You Approve Every Trade Each play waits for your OK. Auto-Send stays off until you turn it on, and even then it only trades within the max price and shares you set.
  • Your Logins Stay on Your Computer They're saved on your own computer and go straight to your brokerage. Nine2Five never sees them.
  • It Signs In Like You Do The bot uses your brokerage's own sign-in and places the same kind of order you would.
  • Stop Any Time Close the app, or switch any account off in Accounts, and it does nothing.

Installation Help

Need a hand installing? Open the steps for your computer.

On a Mac

Three Steps
  1. Open the file you downloaded. Drag the bot's icon onto the Applications folder next to it.
  2. Open it. If your Mac says it can't check the app, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and press Open Anyway.
  3. If your Mac asks to let the bot find devices on local networks, either answer is fine.

On Windows

Three Steps
  1. Open the setup file you downloaded. If a blue box pops up, press More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Press Next until it finishes. No admin password needed.
  3. The bot is now in your Start menu, and on your desktop if you ticked that box.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated November 13, 2026

Nine2Five makes the Nine2Five bot and runs nine2five.live. This page says what we collect, what we never collect, and why.

The Short Version

  • Your brokerage logins stay on your computer. They are never sent to us.
  • The bot sends us only what it needs to check your license and deliver pings, plus a problem report when you choose to send one.
  • nine2five.live has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • We never sell your information.

What Stays on Your Computer

The bot keeps these in its own folder on your computer, and we never receive them:

  • Your brokerage logins, authenticator keys and sign-in sessions.
  • Your accounts, orders, history, profit numbers and settings.

They stay there until you delete the bot's data folder.

What the Bot Sends Us

  • License Check. Your license key and an ID for your computer, scrambled on your computer first so it can't be traced back to your machine. It keeps one key on one computer. The check runs when the bot opens and every 30 minutes. Our license server also uses your internet address to limit how many checks one address can make.
  • Pings. To receive plays, the bot connects to our relay server with a pass from the license check. The relay notes your Whop username and the last time your copy connected.
  • Updates. The bot reads a small file on nine2five.live to see if a new version is out.
  • Problem Reports. Only when you press Send in Report a Problem. A report holds the bot's version, your kind of computer, each brokerage's sign-in status and last error, your trading mode, your last few orders with their results, recent activity lines and any note you type. Before it leaves your computer, the bot removes your saved logins and anything that looks like a password, token, key, full account number, email address or phone number. Our relay keeps only the newest 200 reports, and we can delete any report sooner.

What We Get From Whop

You join and pay through Whop, and Whop handles your payment details. We never see your card. Whop tells us whether your membership is active and shares the account details it gives sellers, such as your username and email. Whop's own privacy policy covers what Whop does.

Discord

If you join the Nine2Five Discord, Discord's privacy policy covers your account there. We see what you post in our server, like any server owner.

nine2five.live

  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Your light or dark choice is saved in your own browser.
  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare, its font comes from Fontshare and the downloads come from GitHub. They see standard request details, like your internet address, to deliver the page and files.

How We Use It

Only to run Nine2Five: checking licenses, delivering pings, fixing problems you report and answering you. We don't sell, rent or share your information for advertising.

Who Else Sees It

Only the services that run Nine2Five: Whop for memberships and payments, Cloudflare for our site, relay and license server, GitHub for the downloads, and Discord if you join. We also share information when the law requires it.

Your Choices

  • Ask what we have about you, or ask us to delete it, by messaging us on Whop or in the Nine2Five Discord.
  • Sign out of the bot on this computer in Settings, under License.
  • Delete the bot's data folder to remove everything it saved on your computer.
  • Cancel your membership on Whop at any time.

Age

Nine2Five is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.

Contact

Message us on Whop or in the Nine2Five Discord. The rules for using Nine2Five are in our Terms of Service.
